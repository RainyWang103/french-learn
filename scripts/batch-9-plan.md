# Phase 3 — Batch 9 Curriculum Plan (Days 57–63)

## Context

Batch 9 is the final batch of Phase 1 (A1). Days 1–56 are already
built. Calendar days 57–63 include revision day 60, so there are
**6 content days** to plan (57, 58, 59, 61, 62, 63) and **1 revision
stub** (day060.json).

All grammar points in the "still available" hint list (`il y a`,
telling time, c'est vs il est, stressed pronouns, venir) have
already been covered in earlier batches — see the coverage sweep
below. Batch 9 therefore introduces fresh A1 grammar and closes out
remaining A1 vocab domains (professions, nationalities,
celebrations, technology, emergencies, social plans).
